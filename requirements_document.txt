PROFESSIONAL BACKGROUND (Home Page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dr. Mohamed Hasain N holds a doctorate in Transportation Engineering from the
National Institute of Technology Silchar, India. His research interests include
traffic safety analysis, crash modelling, transportation planning, and direct
demand modelling using machine learning techniques. He was awarded the University
Silver Medal for academic excellence in his master's program at NIT Silchar.
Dr. Hasain is currently serving as a Post-Doctoral Researcher at the
Transportation Systems Engineering Laboratory, Department of Civil Engineering,
Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. He has been actively involved in several
research projects aimed at improving safe and secure first- and last-mile
connectivity, particularly for pedestrians, in metro transit systems, as well as
assessing the resilience of suburban rail networks in emerging countries in
response to rainfall events.
